# Research Interests

- Large-eddy simulations reduced-order modeling (LES-ROMs)
  - Data-driven regularized reduced-order models (d2-Reg-ROMs)
  - Data-driven eddy viscosity reduced order models (d2-EV-ROMs)
  - Data-driven variational multi-scale reduced-oder models (d2-VMS-ROM)
- Reduced-order modeling for turbulent flows
- Machine Learning
- High-performance computing
- Numerical analysis
